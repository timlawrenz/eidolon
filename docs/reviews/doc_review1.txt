# Eidolon Documentation Review

## Part 1 — What has actually been built and achieved

**Shipped artifacts:**
- **A frozen, pose-invariant geometry encoder (`z_g`)** — 3D-frontalized (EPnP-style, z_scale=1.0), fit on 69,851 FFHQ faces, k=50, 99.99% retained variance, with whitening stats and canonical template persisted inside the `.npz` for reproducible inference. This is the one durable *product* of the project so far.
- **A gate harness** — a reviewed ~100-identity / ~1,500-image hegre verification corpus, a verification-AUC instrument (replacing a mathematically broken trace-J gate), nuisance audits, and systematic-review scripts. This infrastructure is arguably more valuable than the encoder.
- **A settled conditioning decision** — identity from flesh-masked DINOv3 patch pooling (AUC 0.797, cross-shoot controlled, bootstrapped), interpretable control from `z_g` only.

**Achieved knowledge (mostly negative, honestly logged):**
- `z_d` (depth) is dead: negative AUC deltas in every mode, confirmed at 24× facial resolution with domain shift removed.
- `z_a` (normals) is dead: its editorial-corpus PASS was overturned once the artificially weak z_g baseline (keypoint-resolution artifact, 0.540 → 0.671) was corrected.
- The DINOv3→sliders bridge is dead in both directions: R² fails for surface components, and the projection destroys identity (bridge ≤ random 50-d projections of its own input).
- Methodological catches along the way: the contamination near-miss, the circular synthetic-pose probe, the trace-J additivity bug, the de-rotation visibility-bias paradox, the seg-collapse defect, confidence ≠ precision.

**The honest net position:** the founding thesis — `E = [z_g | z_d | z_a] ∈ ℝ^150` as a single self-sufficient person descriptor — is two-thirds falsified. What survives is a 50-d geometry control vector plus an off-the-shelf foundation-model representation for identity. That is a legitimate research outcome, but it should be named: the project's headline architecture did not survive contact with its own gates.

## Part 2 — Shortcomings in prose and document state

### 2.1 `architecture.md` is materially stale and now contradicts the ledger
This is the most serious documentation problem. The architecture doc still asserts, as current truth:
- §4: z_a marked **(ACTIVE)**, and the "Macro architectural finding" that z_a (0.562) beats z_g (0.540) "carries the biological identity" and should be "the identity-dominant partition for DiT conditioning." **Every part of this was overturned on 2026-06-11** — the 0.540 baseline was an artifact, and z_a *subtracts* signal on face crops (−0.039).
- §0/§7: `E ∈ ℝ^150` with three partitions and `n_parts=3` block-diagonal ingestion, including an `mlp_d` for a partition declared conclusively dead.
- §5: the bridge R² is "the single most important number in the project" and §8 calls it the "project go/no-go signal." The bridge failed; the project (sensibly) did not stop — but the doc never reconciles this. A fresh reader would conclude the project is dead by its own criterion.

A reader who opens `architecture.md` — nominally the definitive design document — reconstructs a project state that is roughly 24 hours and three major reversals out of date.

### 2.2 The ledger's append-only structure has decayed into misdirection
`02_EXPERIMENTS_AND_RESULTS.md` has genuine structural defects:
- **Two Phase 3 sections coexist** — one `[ACTIVE]`/`[PENDING]`, one `[CONCLUDED]` — with no supersession marker on the first.
- **Phase 2's evidence is scattered across three locations**: the section header still says `[ACTIVE]`, its verdict says "CONCLUDED — FAIL (above)" — but the gate-run table, metric-bug analysis, and artifacts block physically sit *below*, orphaned inside and after the Phase 3 and Phase 4 sections. Navigating by headers attributes z_d's results to the wrong phase. The "(above)" pointer is simply wrong.
- **Inconsistent supersession practice.** Phase 1 does it right: "**PASS (REVOKED — see Phase 1-R below)**." But Phase 2b's verdict still reads "**[CONCLUDED — z_a PASSES]**" with the proven-thesis prose intact, while the overturn lives inside the *Phase 2* section under a 2026-06-11 update. There is no forward pointer at the stale PASS. Anyone reading the Phase 2b section in isolation walks away believing the opposite of the project's conclusion.
- Minor: duplicate "3." numbering in the Phase 3 findings list; `"reaon_method"` typo in `zd_gate_results.json`.

### 2.3 Cross-document numeric conflicts
- **Phase 3b controls are quoted from two different runs without flagging.** The tree says raw cls = 0.685, random projections = 0.627 ± 0.006, bridge = 0.606 (matching the persisted JSONs). The ledger says 0.766 / 0.712 ± 0.007 / 0.704 (the face-crop re-run, for which **no artifact exists**). The tree then uses *both* sets in adjacent bullets. The qualitative conclusion is the same, but a careful reader cannot determine which numbers are canonical.
- **The z_g face-crop baseline floats**: 0.671 (correction table), 0.681/0.689 (z_d re-run), 0.688 (z_a overturn) — presumably different subsets/filters, but never reconciled; the ledger papers over it with "0.67–0.69."
- **Full face-crop set size floats**: 1,429 vs 1,448 vs 1,460 images across sections, unexplained. For a project that nearly got killed by a labeling artifact, sample accounting is surprisingly loose.

### 2.4 Terminology and framing
- **`z_a` is called "albedo" throughout, but it is built from surface normals.** Albedo is reflectance; normals are geometry. This is not pedantry — "albedo/surface" implies the partition captures appearance information it never touched, and "volumetric" for normals is similarly loose. Any external reader will be misled.
- **The North Star says "face, albedo, and body."** Nothing in the project touches the body — 68 facial keypoints, face crops, facial depth/normals. The scope narrowed from "person" to "face" silently.
- **Rhetorical register.** "FATAL", "DIED at scale", "stunningly high-resolution", "THE PIVOT". This is fine for a private notebook, but it does two harmful things: it pre-frames conclusions before evidence is shown, and it ages badly — the Phase 2b headline confidence reads as an indictment after the overturn. Notably, the overclaiming headlines correlate with the verdicts that later flipped.

### 2.5 Literature synthesis never back-annotated
`01_LITERATURE_SYNTHESIS.md` still validates the three-modality design and states "the stratum-hq → Sapiens pipeline for z_d is validated" — which aged badly within 48 hours. Citation hygiene is inconsistent (several NeurIPS entries lack authors), "30 years of 3DMM research" is ~27 (Blanz & Vetter 1999), and the synthesis's "what the literature does NOT support" section makes stronger claims than its sources do (Rolinek shows VAEs *tend toward* PCA directions under specific conditions; it doesn't establish PCA ≥ learned latents for downstream control).

## Part 3 — Shortcomings in the scientific evidence

### 3.1 Every decision rests on one small, single-source corpus
~100 identities, ~1,400–1,700 images, all from a single editorial photography site with a demographically narrow subject pool (young adult female models, professional lighting). Every kill/keep decision — z_d dead, z_a dead, DINO wins, patch pooling wins — is underwritten by this one corpus. There is no second source, no external benchmark (even a small LFW/IJB-style verification subset would do), and yet the conclusions are stated as universals.

### 3.2 The flagship mechanism claim is confounded and overstated
"**Monocular volumetric models hallucinate generic, plausible human geometry**" is a mechanism claim. The evidence is: *50-d PCA projections* of (64×64-resampled) Sapiens outputs add no verification AUC. But `za_fit_summary.json` shows the z_a PCA basis retains only **66–75% of variance** (vs 99.99% for z_g) — a quarter to a third of the normal-map signal is discarded *before* the gate ever sees it, and high-frequency micro-curvature is exactly what PCA truncation discards first. No raw-pixel control, no k≫50 run, no nonlinear probe was performed on the normals before declaring the fast path "definitively dead." The z_d write-up was properly careful here ("What is NOT ruled out"); the z_a overturn abandoned that care. The supporting evidence offered — "visual inspection confirmed stunningly high-resolution, topologically accurate" — is subjective and actually cuts *against* the hallucination thesis being cleanly established. "Dead as currently encoded" is supported; "monocular models do not encode identity" is not.

### 3.3 Uncertainty handling matured only at the end — and the early gates failed exactly as that predicts
Pre-Phase-4 gates quantify noise via 3 seeds of *pair resampling*, which captures sampling noise of the pairs but not uncertainty over the ~100 identities. The z_a PASS cleared its ε by "2.4–2.8×" and was then overturned wholesale when the measurement substrate changed — empirical proof the stated error bars understated true uncertainty. Identity-level bootstrap CIs appear only in Phase 4. Worse: the **z_scale=1.0 production decision rests on a 10-identity, 136-image Fisher table with no uncertainty estimates**, whose machine-readable artifact is corrupt and deliberately not regenerated, and the sweep was never re-run on the 100-identity corpus. A frozen, shipped encoder parameter is permanently evidenced by the thinnest table in the project.

### 3.4 Pre-registration is claimed but not honored in the strict sense
The "stated BEFORE results — honest-science discipline" framing is undercut by the record:
- The Phase 2 gate (trace-J ×1.15) was replaced mid-phase (legitimately — the metric was broken, and the cross-examination was excellent).
- The Phase 3 criterion (C1–C10 ≥ 0.6) was disavowed post-hoc via the C6-noise probe — yet the **FAIL verdict still stands on a criterion the same document calls "partly wrong."**
- The Phase 3b gate "passed" and was then declared structurally too weak.
- The Phase 3 pre-registered falsifiable prediction ("z_a will have a *strictly lower* R² spectrum than z_g") is **never evaluated** — and it's actually false component-wise (z_a C1 R²=0.750 > z_g C1 0.652; only the weighted aggregate is lower). Registering a falsifiable prediction and not scoring it defeats the purpose.

Each individual revision is well-argued. But after three mid-flight gate redesigns, the pre-registration language conveys more rigor than the workflow delivers.

### 3.5 Undisclosed thresholds and selection effects
- The nuisance audit's CLEAN/SUSPECT boundary is never defined. `xy` is labeled CLEAN with a max |corr| of **0.47** against pitch in its top-5 components (per `za_gate_results.json`). By what threshold is 0.47 clean? The number is in the artifact; the criterion is nowhere.
- Multiple comparisons are unaddressed: 4 z_a variants, 3 z_d modes × re-standardization × ablations, a 4-point z-scale sweep — best-of selection on Δ ≈ 0.02 effects without selection-adjusted uncertainty. The "all variants pass" robustness in z_a mitigated this once; the practice is otherwise unexamined.
- Pre-Phase-4 verification pairs were not shoot-controlled, so the z_a editorial PASS could have included shoot-context leakage — moot after the overturn, but it means the gate that approved z_a was weaker than the gate that later killed it, on two axes simultaneously (baseline artifact + pair composition).

### 3.6 The evidence trail thins precisely where the stakes peak
Everything in `docs/assets/` ends at the 2026-06-10 state. The 2026-06-11 work — the z_d face-crop confirmation, the **z_a overturn**, the **z_g correction**, and **Phase 4** (the project's only currently-positive result) — has **zero preserved artifacts** under `docs/assets/`; the ledger tables are the sole record. Meanwhile, stale artifacts that contradict final verdicts sit unannotated: `za_gate_results.json` says `"overall_verdict": "PASS", "best_variant": "rot"` (overturned, and rot was rejected even within the PASS); `phase3b_transfer_results.json` says `"verdict": "PASS"` with an undocumented, nonsensical `"fraction_identity_retained": 1.71`. By the project's own convention (evidence preserved in `docs/assets/<branch>/`), the most decision-critical results are the least evidenced.

### 3.7 The invariance principle is applied selectively
Pose was disqualified from `z_g` on definitional grounds: "identity is invariant; pose is transient." Expression is equally transient — yet Phase 1 celebrates **C4 = mouth opening** as a clean component, the production encoder retains expression variance, and no de-expression analog or gate exists. The gates quietly absorb expression into within-identity scatter (acknowledged once as "S_W inflation"). On the architecture's own definition of E, this is an unresolved internal inconsistency, not an edge case.

### 3.8 Smaller statistical points
- "The bridge is *worse than* a random projection of its own input": in the re-run, 0.704 vs 0.712 ± 0.007 is a ~1σ gap. "No better than any 50-d shadow" is solid; "worse than" outruns the stats (the older run's ~3σ gap is cleaner, but then the runs are mixed — see 2.3).
- §2's "perfectly isolated sliders... zero linear correlation" holds in-sample only; the project's own data shows the whitening drifting badly out of distribution (per-component std 1.97 on editorial). And the illustrative "C_1 raw variance ≈ 1000" is impossible for [-1,1]-normalized coordinates — invented numbers in an otherwise precision-obsessed document.

## Part 4 — What the documentation does well (for balance)

- **Negative results are logged permanently and prominently** — rare and genuinely valuable.
- **The self-correction record is exceptional**: a revoked PASS, an abandoned circular probe, a confessed metric bug found by cross-examining four metrics, a corrected understated baseline, the confidence≠precision lesson. Most research notebooks bury these.
- **The trace-J additivity analysis and the visibility-bias paradox are both mathematically correct, non-obvious catches**, and the paradox numbers in the prose (−0.008 → −0.935) exactly match the persisted JSON.
- **Phase 4 is the methodological high-water mark** — pre-registered control arm, identity-level bootstrap, cross-shoot-only instrument, engineering audits (patch-layout verification, visual alignment proof) *before* accepting the result. If earlier phases had been run at Phase 4's standard, the z_a PASS likely never happens.
- The contamination forensics, with collages preserved as evidence, is exemplary.

---

## Bottom line

As a **lab notebook**, this is well above par: the failure analysis is honest, the bug-hunting is real, and rigor visibly improves phase over phase. As **documentation of record**, it currently fails its reader in two ways: (1) the cross-document state is inconsistent enough that `architecture.md` or any single ledger section read in isolation yields materially false conclusions — the stale z_a "identity-dominant partition" claim being the worst offender; and (2) the project's strongest universal claim — volumetrics are dead because monocular models hallucinate — is a mechanism conclusion drawn from a compression-confounded null on a single narrow corpus, with its decisive runs unpreserved. The weakest evidence in the repo sits under the boldest prose.
