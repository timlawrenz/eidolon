## Documentation Review: Project Eidolon (`docs/`)

### Overall Structure

Four documents, clearly separated by function: literature synthesis (01), architecture (02), experiment ledger (03), and experiment tree (03). The division of labor is sound. Approximately 50 supporting artifacts live under `docs/assets/exp/geometry-pca/` (plots, JSON metric exports, collages), giving the prose a paper trail. That is a strength.

### Document-by-Document Assessment

#### `01_LITERATURE_SYNTHESIS.md` — Solid base, one blind spot

**Prose:** Clean. The five-pillar structure (PCA orthogonality, block-diagonal conditioning, DiT architectures, 3DMM lineage, data pipeline) is navigable. The ShapeFusion deep-dive with the comparison table and the tiered reference trace are genuinely useful — this is not a generic dump of abstracts, it is curated against Eidolon's thesis.

**Scientific evidence:** The seven-point research synthesis (lines 136–178) correctly identifies the gap Eidolon occupies. The "critical assessment" section honestly lists what the literature does NOT support and what remains unproven. Good discipline.

**Shortcomings:**

1. **Missing the monocular hallucination problem.** The single most important empirical finding from the experiment ledger — that Sapiens depth/normals are geometrically *plausible* but not *identity-specific*, i.e., monocular volumetric models hallucinate generic human geometry — has no literature context. There is a body of work on the limits of monocular depth estimation for biometric identity. The synthesis should discuss it, because it is now the explanatory theory for why both z_d and z_a failed.

2. **The "3-modality" framing is outdated.** The synthesis asks whether "3-modality ingestion is sufficient" — the experiments conclusively show it is *one modality too many*. The synthesis was written before Phase 2b's face-crop overturn; it should be updated to reflect that only geometry (z_g) survived as a structured partition, with DINO semantic tokens carrying identity.

3. **No masked-patch / token-pooling literature connection.** Phase 4 (masked DINO patch pooling) has no explicit literature anchor in this document. There is relevant work on masked attention / region-pooled ViT features that could ground it.

### Summary of What Has Been Built and Achieved

Distilled from all four documents, the empirical record shows:

1. **z_g (geometry encoder):** Built, shipped, and survived. A frozen 50-d PCA encoder on 69,851 FFHQ faces with 3D frontalization (z_scale=1.0). Pose-invariant by construction. On face-crop keypoints, verification AUC ≈ 0.67–0.69. It is a moderate identity carrier and the sole surviving structured partition.

2. **z_d (depth encoder):** Built and conclusively killed. Monocular relative depth at k=50 adds no complementary identity signal — in fact it *subtracts* (ΔAUC −0.023 to −0.034). Resolution and domain shift are exhausted as excuses. The trace-J metric bug was caught and the gate instrument was corrected to verification AUC.

3. **z_a (surface normals encoder):** Built, initially passed, then overturned on face-crop re-run. The initial PASS was an artifact of the low editorial-keypoint z_g baseline. At proper resolution, normals dilute identity (ΔAUC −0.039). The 2.5D rotation trap (visibility bias) was discovered and documented as a permanent architectural warning.

4. **DINOv3 bridge:** Built and killed. Linear regression cannot faithfully reconstruct physical sliders from DINO embeddings. The bridge degrades identity beyond random projections of its own input. Both fast-path directions are dead. Raw DINOv3 CLS on face crops is the strongest identity carrier measured (AUC 0.766), with same-shoot leakage as a caveat.

5. **Masked DINO patch tokens (Phase 4):** Built and passed. Flesh-masked mean-pooled DINOv3 patches beat the CLS baseline (AUC 0.797 vs 0.769). The +0.027 lift is pure cross-shoot identity signal. This is the settled identity representation for DiT conditioning.

6. **Infrastructure:** A reusable verification-AUC gate instrument, a growing identity review corpus (`review.db`, 100+ verified clean identities), a suite of ~37 scripts with tests, and the block-diagonal ingestion module spec (the `BlockDiagonalIngestion` class in architecture.md §7.1 — designed but not yet trained).

**Net result:** The project has empirically narrowed E from three structured partitions to one (z_g), killed two plausible-but-wrong paths (monocular volumetrics, DINO→slider regression), and identified the correct identity carrier (masked DINO patches). Phase 5 (DiT fusion stack) is the next build target. The architecture document needs a rewrite to reflect this.
