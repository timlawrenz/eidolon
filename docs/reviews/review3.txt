Based on a review of the repository's documentation (specifically `02_EXPERIMENTS_AND_RESULTS.md` and `03_EXPERIMENT_TREE.md`), here is a summary of what has been built and achieved, along with an objective critique of the prose and scientific evidence.

### What Has Been Built and Achieved

The Eidolon project aimed to construct a decoupled identity vector `E = [z_g | z_d | z_a]` to condition a Diffusion Transformer (DiT), separating physical morphology from semantic identity. Over four phases, the team iteratively tested and discarded representations, arriving at a final architecture:

1. **Phase 1-R (Geometry Encoder `z_g`):** A functional 50-dimensional pose-invariant geometry encoder was built. It uses an EPnP-style orthographic 3D frontalization of 68 2D facial keypoints (DWPose) to strip out transient camera orientation (yaw/pitch) before applying PCA. It achieves an identity verification AUC of ~0.67–0.69.
2. **Phase 2 & 2b (Volumetric and Surface Encoders `z_d`, `z_a`):** The project built infrastructure to extract depth and normal maps via the Sapiens monocular network. However, exhaustive testing proved that these models hallucinate generic human geometry. Neither depth nor normal maps added complementary, identity-specific biological signal over 2D geometry. The volumetric "fast path" was conclusively abandoned.
3. **Phase 3 (DINOv3 Bridge):** A Ridge Regression bridge mapping DINOv3 semantic embeddings to the physical sliders was built. The experiment proved that semantic foundation models cannot reliably reconstruct fine geometric features (R² for surface curvature was 0.385), proving the geometric sliders are non-redundant. 
4. **Phase 4 (Semantic Face Isolation):** The team successfully engineered a "Masked Average Pooling" technique. By pooling only the 16x16 DINOv3 patches that fall strictly inside a flesh-segmentation mask, they isolated biological identity from shoot/lighting context, achieving a peak verification AUC of 0.797.
5. **Final Architecture:** The project simplified the DiT conditioning stack to two paths: **Flesh-masked DINOv3 patches** (for raw identity) + **`z_g`** (for interpretable geometric control).

### Shortcomings in Prose

While the documentation is highly transparent about failures, the writing style deviates significantly from standard scientific neutrality:
* **Overly Dramatic and Emotional Phrasing:** The ledger relies heavily on sensational language (e.g., "The control that kills the story," "amputate a mathematically sound 3D frontalization step," "The counter-intuitive reality," "stunningly high-resolution"). 
* **Manifesto-like Tone:** The text often moralizes its methodological updates (e.g., emphasizing "honest-science discipline" and "the cold hard truth"). This editorializing makes the document read like a post-mortem blog post or narrative rather than an objective engineering log. 

### Shortcomings in Scientific Evidence & Methodology

The documentation actively logs several severe methodological oversights that temporarily corrupted the project's scientific evidence. While most were eventually caught, they highlight a pattern of weak initial experimental design:

1. **Self-Fulfilling Synthetic Probes (Phase 1-R):** The initial proof of pose-invariance relied on a synthetic probe that was mathematically circular. It used a depth prior to lift 2D points into 3D, and then tested pose-normalization using that exact same depth model, testing algebraic reversibility rather than true biological invariance.
2. **Fundamentally Flawed Metrics (Phase 2):** The project initially used the "Trace Fisher Ratio" (`trace-J`) as its gate instrument to measure complementarity between concatenated vectors. This was mathematically invalid, as `trace-J` for concatenated vectors acts as a weighted average and is inherently blind to orthogonal/complementary information. 
3. **Missing Null Hypotheses (Phase 3b):** A transfer test was initially marked as a "pass" based on absolute AUC, but failed to include a random-projection null. A later control proved that a completely random 50-d projection of DINO outperformed the trained regression bridge, rendering the initial test uninformative.
4. **Baseline Discrepancies and Artificial Positives (Phases 2b & 1-R):** Surface normals (`z_a`) initially "passed" the gate because they were compared against an artificially weak geometry baseline. The geometry baseline had been evaluated on low-resolution editorial frames, while the normal maps were evaluated on high-resolution face crops. Correcting this resolution mismatch overturned the result.
5. **Data Contamination and Underpowered Tests:** The early Phase 1 dataset suffered from identity collisions (e.g., male partners detected in "single person" bounding boxes). Additionally, in Phase 4, the "Shoot-Leakage Probe" was rendered moot because the dataset only contained 41 same-identity/same-shoot pairs—far too few to achieve statistical power.
