Eidolon — Recommendations for Continuation
The strategic situation in one paragraph
The original thesis — E = [z_g|z_d|z_a] as a self-sufficient person descriptor — is two-thirds falsified by your own gates, and what survives (frozen pose-invariant z_g + flesh-masked DINOv3 patches) is a different and still defensible project: interpretable, orthogonal geometric control over a foundation-model-conditioned generator, with firewalled fusion. The field has meanwhile converged on adjacent territory: Arc2Face (ECCV 2024 oral) generates from ArcFace embeddings alone; InstantID combines an FR embedding with keypoint spatial control; DiffusionRig/RigFace condition diffusion on 3DMM "rigs"; Concept Sliders and PreciseControl deliver post-hoc interpretable sliders on pretrained models. Nobody does orthogonal-by-construction, pre-registered, gate-validated slider conditioning — that, plus your negative-results methodology, is the remaining novelty. But two measurement debts and one architectural inconsistency should be settled before any DiT compute is spent.

Tier 0 — Measurement debts to close first (days, CPU-mostly, no training)
0.1 Run the ArcFace/AdaFace baseline on the hegre gate. This is the most important missing number in the project.
The ledger calls raw DINOv3 (AUC 0.797) "the strongest identity carrier measured" — but only stratum-derived layers were ever measured. The field's standard identity signal is a dedicated FR embedding (ArcFace/AdaFace via InsightFace, pip-installable, minutes for 1,500 images). On editorial face crops it will very likely land ≥0.9, far above 0.797.
Why this matters beyond bookkeeping: Arc2Face and InstantID both demonstrate that ArcFace embeddings are sufficient and superior conditioning for ID-consistent generation. If ArcFace dominates on your own gate, the honest conditioning stack becomes identity = ArcFace (512-d), appearance/texture = DINO patches, geometry control = z_g — three streams, which conveniently means your block-diagonal/decoupled-attention design survives intact with reassigned roles. If you skip this measurement and DINO patches underperform as the identity anchor in Phase 5, you won't know whether the firewall failed or the conditioning signal was just weak.

Also re-scope the ledger claim either way ("strongest measured among stratum layers").

0.2 Resolve the pose double-conditioning inconsistency you created for yourselves.
z_g was rebuilt at great cost to be the "pose-orthogonal complement" of pose.npy, on the argument that double-conditioning pose is a disqualifying optimization conflict. But the chosen identity stream — DINO patch tokens of a posed image — carries head pose far more strongly than C₁ ever did. The project's own logic is currently applied to the 50-d partition and waived for the 1,261×1024 one.
Cheap probe (an afternoon): linear-regress estimated yaw/pitch from the pooled flesh-masked vector (and from a patch-token subsample). If pose is strongly decodable — it will be — make an explicit, documented decision: accept entanglement in the identity stream (the IP-Adapter/Arc2Face world does, and it works), and retire "pose-orthogonal complement" as a system-wide requirement, keeping it as a z_g-specific property that enables clean sliders. What you should not do is leave the contradiction implicit in architecture.md.

0.3 Split z_g into morphology vs. transient sliders using machinery you already have.
My review flagged that expression is transient-by-your-own-definition yet lives in z_g (C4 = mouth opening). You already compute per-component Fisher J on the hegre multi-image corpus (J_Ci in zd_gate_results.json). Rank all 50 components by within/between-identity variance ratio and tag each slider as morphology (high J) or transient (low J). Zero new infrastructure, and it directly feeds the Phase 5 gates (see 1.4): transient sliders must preserve FR identity when moved; morphology sliders are expected to change it. The literature is converging on exactly this decomposition (Arc2Face's FLAME-blendshape expression adapter, FaceCrafter's pose/expression/emotion split), so this also future-proofs an expression-control stream.
0.4 Add one external, pose-controlled verification check.
Every conclusion currently rests on one editorial corpus. Cheapest credible fix: a frontal–profile pairs benchmark (CFP-style, public) or FaceScape (free academic application, 847 subjects × 20 expressions, real 3D ground truth) to (a) test z_g's pose-invariance against real multi-view data — closing the "synthetic probe was circular" caveat permanently — and (b) check the DINO-vs-z_g ordering replicates off-hegre. Multi-PIE is the gold standard but licensed; don't block on it.
0.5 Pay the documentation debt before new work lands on top of it.
From my review, the minimum set: rewrite architecture.md §0/§4/§5/§7 to the post-overturn state (z_a/z_d dead, E re-scoped, n_parts no longer 3, bridge-R² no longer the "go/no-go signal"); add supersession markers on the stale Phase 2b PASS; reunify the orphaned Phase 2 evidence; preserve the 2026-06-11 face-crop artifacts (z_a overturn, z_g correction, Phase 4) under docs/assets/ — the project's only positive result currently has no machine-readable evidence; define the CLEAN/SUSPECT nuisance threshold; back-annotate the literature synthesis (its "stratum→Sapiens pipeline for z_d is validated" and three-modality framing aged badly within 48 hours).
Tier 1 — Phase 5 design, informed by the field
1.1 Train an adapter on a frozen pretrained backbone first; defer the from-scratch DiT.
The lit synthesis's own canonical reference shows the way: IP-Adapter achieved fine-tuning-level conditioning with 22M trainable parameters on a frozen base — that fits the 4090 with room to spare. A from-scratch DiT on 70k FFHQ images is both compute-hungry (MicroDiT's "$1,890" budget was 8×H100-days, and that was 37M images) and data-poor. The honest trade-off: an adapter rides a powerful prior but inherits its entanglements; from-scratch gives clean architecture claims but mediocre samples that will muddy slider evaluation. Recommendation: adapter-first spike (PixArt-α / SD-class base, decoupled cross-attention adapters for the 2–3 streams, λ sliders) to validate the control thesis cheaply; only escalate to a custom DiT if the frozen prior provably fights the firewall. If you do go from-scratch later, REPA-style alignment is unusually cheap for you — the DINOv3 targets are already cached in stratum for every training image.
1.2 Redesign §7 ingestion for the real, asymmetric streams.
The block-diagonal firewall was specified for three symmetric 50-d partitions. Reality: ~50 z_g scalars (expanded tokens — keep that design, it's sound) vs ~1,261 DINO patch tokens (median) vs possibly one ArcFace token. Keep the principle (independent per-stream projections, summed attention contributions, per-stream λ) and drop the dead mlp_d. Decide patch-token handling explicitly: full set, fixed-size subsample, or perceiver-style resampler (what IP-Adapter-plus does). The "expanded tokens, not a fat token" argument and mandatory whitening both survive unchanged.
1.3 Solve the identity-data problem before training, not during.
FFHQ is ~one image per identity. Reconstruction-style conditioning (condition on E(x) and DINO(x), denoise x) trains fine and suffices for slider claims, but it cannot teach "same person, new image" — Arc2Face needed multi-image-per-ID data (upsampled WebFace42M) for exactly this reason. Options, in order of preference: (a) accept reconstruction training and make identity-generalization an explicitly out-of-scope claim for v1; (b) add a public multi-image-per-ID corpus (availability is messy — VGGFace2 was withdrawn officially; CelebA is accessible but low-res); (c) video-frame identities (CelebV-HQ). Do not train on hegre — it's your gate instrument; using it for training contaminates every downstream evaluation.
1.4 Pre-register the Phase 5 gates now, in the ledger, with the Phase 4-grade methodology.
Concrete proposals, applying your own accumulated lessons (controls, identity-level bootstrap, random/null arms):
Slider obedience (cycle consistency) — traverse z_g component k on a fixed seed → generate → re-extract DWPose keypoints → re-encode with the frozen encoder → measure Δz_g. Gate on an on-target/off-target response ratio (e.g., on-axis response ≥ N× the max off-axis response, averaged over components and seeds). This is the generative analog of your traversal gate and the project's headline metric — nobody in the Concept-Sliders/PreciseControl line reports orthogonality this rigorously.
Transient/morphology dissociation (uses 0.3): moving transient sliders must keep ArcFace similarity above threshold; moving morphology sliders must change ArcFace similarity smoothly and monotonically. Note this gate is impossible to state without the 0.3 tagging — another reason Tier 0 comes first.
The conflict test — the firewall's actual purpose: condition appearance/identity stream from person A, z_g from person B. Re-extracted geometry must track B (keypoint distance), appearance must track A (FR similarity / LPIPS). This cross-conditioning demo, quantified, is the strongest unique artifact Eidolon can produce; no competitor reports it.
Controls: a λ_g=0 arm (sliders unplugged — proves obedience isn't coming from the base prior), a shuffled-z_g null, and at least a qualitative side-by-side against Concept Sliders or DiffusionRig for positioning.
Consider finally using the Holtz et al. unsupervised disentanglement metric the lit synthesis promised as a validation gate and never used — or strike it from the doc.
1.5 Wildcard, clearly labeled exploratory: RAE-style diffusion in DINOv3 latent space.
Representation Autoencoders (ICLR 2026; SigLIP/DINO encoder + trained decoder, DiT diffuses in the representation space) are unusually well-matched to this repo: stratum already stores DINOv3 patches for the entire corpus, so the expensive half of the autoencoder is pre-paid, and "identity conditioning" partially collapses into "the latent space itself." A spike here is a branch-sized bet (decoder training is 4090-feasible at 256px), not the mainline — but if it works it removes the VAE from the stack entirely and aligns the generator's native space with your measurement instruments.
Tier 2 — Positioning and process
Re-scope the public narrative now. The lit-synthesis "unique intersection" claim (three PCA modalities + firewall + DiT) no longer describes the project. The defensible claim set: (1) pose-invariant orthogonal 2D-keypoint slider space, gate-validated; (2) firewalled multi-stream conditioning with per-stream strength control; (3) quantified slider-obedience/conflict metrics; (4) a disciplined negative-results record on monocular volumetrics. Nearest published neighbors to cite and differentiate: DiffusionRig (3DMM rigs, personalized), PreciseControl (W+ space), Concept Sliders (post-hoc LoRA directions), Arc2Face+blendshapes (expression adapter).
Write up the negative results while they're fresh. The z_d/z_a kill chain — trace-J additivity bug, visibility-bias paradox, seg-collapse control, baseline-artifact overturn — is a genuinely publishable methods/negative-results piece (workshop or tech report), and writing it forces the evidence-preservation pass of 0.5. One scope correction first: soften "monocular models hallucinate generic geometry" to "adds no verification signal under PCA-k50 on our corpus" unless you run the raw-pixel/high-k control — your z_a PCA bases retained only 66–75% variance, so a third of the signal was discarded before the gate ever saw it.
Optional cheap baseline: a per-image FLAME shape-coefficient fit (Pixel3DMM makes this nearly turnkey) scored on the same hegre gate would tell you whether the homegrown z_g beats the 30-year-standard control space it implicitly competes with. Framed strictly as a measurement, not a pipeline revival — morphometrics stays deprecated.
